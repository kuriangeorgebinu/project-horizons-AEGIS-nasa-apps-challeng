    NASA OSDR · OSD-575 / OSD-569

    Inspiration4 crew blood data

    2.5–5.9 pg/mL

    Real pre-flight IL-6 range of the 4 civilian astronauts. GENE seeds each crew member's personal baseline inside it.

    Tested: replayed through GENE: 3 of 4 real post-flight IL-6 rises caught, 1 false alarm in 12 pre-flight samples. Caveat: the rule was tuned on this same data, so this is a consistency check, not an independent test.
    osdr.nasa.gov · OSD-575 ↗
    Curiosity RAD · Zeitlin et al. 2013, Science

    Radiation on the cruise to Mars

    1.84 mSv / day

    Measured inside the spacecraft on the way to Mars: the background SOLARIS judges every reading against (warning 2×, shelter 5×).

    Tested: 8 of 8 measured environments and regression cases judged correctly (the old fixed rule got 3 of 8)
    doi.org/10.1126/science.1235989 ↗
    Curiosity RAD · Hassler 2014 · Chang'e-4 LND · Zhang 2020

    Mars surface and the Moon

    0.64 · 1.37 mSv / day

    Measured on the surface of Mars (Gale crater) and the Moon: the backgrounds for the Mars-surface and lunar mission profiles.

    Tested: no false alarms on either surface; the old fixed rule would have ordered a storm shelter every day on the Moon
    Mars ↗ Moon ↗
    NASA-STD-3001 · NASA OCHMO · Human Research Roadmap

    NASA limits and risks

    600 mSv career · 250 mGy-Eq / 30 days

    NASA's radiation limits (600 mSv career effective dose; 250 mGy-Eq to the blood-forming organs in any 30 days, the short-term limit that matters in a storm), plus the countermeasures and Human Research Program risks the AI must cite when it gives advice.

    Tested: the fact checker caught 21 of 21 invented numbers, 45 of 45 fake citations and 45 of 45 invented medicines
    humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov ↗
